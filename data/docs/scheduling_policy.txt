Appointment Scheduling Policy

Patients can book same-day or future appointments through the voice copilot.
Available appointment types are: general consultation (20 min), follow-up
(15 min), and urgent care (30 min, same-day only).

Clinic hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM
to 1:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

To book an appointment, the copilot needs: the patient's name, a preferred
day, and a brief reason for the visit (used only to route to the right
appointment type and provider, not for diagnosis). Same-day urgent slots
are limited and are offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

Cancellations require at least 4 hours notice to avoid a missed-appointment
fee. Rescheduling can be done directly through the copilot at any time
before the appointment.

For anything requiring immediate medical attention, patients are always
instructed to call emergency services or go to the nearest emergency room
rather than booking a routine appointment.
