General Clinic Information (Non-Diagnostic)

This copilot provides general clinic information only. It does not
diagnose conditions, recommend medications, or replace advice from a
licensed clinician. For any specific symptom, medication question, or
concern about your health, the copilot will always recommend speaking
with a doctor or nurse directly.

What the copilot CAN help with:
- Explaining appointment types and what to expect during a visit
- Clinic hours, location, and insurance/billing basics
- General wellness information (e.g., the value of regular check-ups,
  what a follow-up visit typically covers)
- Scheduling, rescheduling, or canceling appointments

What the copilot will NOT do:
- Diagnose a condition based on described symptoms
- Recommend or adjust medication dosages
- Provide emergency guidance (patients experiencing a medical emergency
  are always directed to call emergency services immediately)

The goal of the copilot is to reduce administrative back-and-forth for
routine, non-urgent needs so clinical staff can focus on patient care.
