Visit Types and General Wellness Information

General consultation: a 20-minute visit for new or ongoing non-urgent
concerns. The clinician will review history, discuss symptoms at a high
level, and decide on next steps (tests, referrals, or follow-up).

Follow-up visit: a 15-minute check-in after a prior visit, test, or
procedure, to review progress and adjust the care plan if needed.

Urgent care: a same-day, 30-minute visit for concerns that need prompt
attention but are not medical emergencies. True emergencies should always
go to an emergency room or call emergency services rather than booking
through this system.

Regular check-ups are generally recommended on an annual basis for adults,
though frequency depends on age, history, and clinician guidance. Staying
current on preventive visits is one of the most effective ways to catch
issues early, but the copilot does not set or override that schedule —
it only helps book the visit type your clinician recommends.
